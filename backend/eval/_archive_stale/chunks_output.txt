----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Crop: Sugarcane (ಕಬ್ಬು / Saccharum officinarum L. Poaceae) | Topic: Industrial Processing And Byproducts
Standard Yield: 100t of cane produces 10t sugar + 4t molasses + 3t filter mud + 0.3t ash + 30t bagasse
Bagasse: Used as fuel, for making fibre boards and paper (7t bagasse = 1t paper).
Molasses: Used in distilleries for ethyl alcohol (ethanol), blended with petrol (Gasohol). Used for oxalic, maleic, and citric acid, Rum, and livestock feed additive.
Press Mud: Rich in organic 'C', 'P', & 'S'; used in vermicomposting as manure.
Green Tops: Good sources of fodder.
Co Generation: A plant of 2500 TCD generates 7-8 MW surplus power.

CHUNK 4
CATEGORY: general
TOPIC: Botany And Growth
SIZE: 1063
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Crop: Sugarcane (ಕಬ್ಬು / Saccharum officinarum L. Poaceae) | Topic: Botany And Growth
Plant Type: Perennial grass growing in tropical regions.
Morphology: 3-4 m height, tillering, unbranching stalk (cane). Stalk has distinct nodes and internodes coated with waxy material. Eye bud at each node protected by leaf sheath.
Tillering: Produces 20-40 tillers per cane from below soil surface.
Inflorescence: Called 'arrow', has male and female parts. Produced at 10-12 months (qualitative short day plant).
Root System:
Sett Roots: Temporary roots from root primordia at the base of every cane joint; provide initial moisture and nutrients.
Shoot Roots: Permanent, thick fleshy white roots from lower nodes of the primary shoot; provide anchorage.
Flowering Disadvantages: Reduces quality (1% Sucrose loss), pithy canes, loss of apical dominance, development of water shoots, shorter leaf blade, low yield (25% loss), high fibre, reduced juice extraction.
Flowering Control: Withholding water, mechanical defoliation, chemical sprays (Maleic hydrazide - MH, ethephon).

TOPIC: Climate And Soil
SIZE: 903
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Crop: Sugarcane (ಕಬ್ಬು / Saccharum officinarum L. Poaceae) | Topic: Climate And Soil
Title Kannada: ಮಣ್ಣಿನ ತಯಾರಿ ಮತ್ತು ಭೂ ನಿರ್ವಹಣೆ
Climate: Tropical to subtropical. Optimum temperature: 21-35°C (Growth arrests if <20°C or >50°C). Cold stress below 12°C.
Rainfall: Well distributed annual rainfall of 750-1200 mm is optimum. Total water requirement: 1200-1500 mm.
Sunshine: Bright sunshine leads to thicker, shorter stems and broader greener leaves. Low sunshine leads to slender, long stems with narrow yellowish leaves.
Day Length: Short day length decreases tillers and tonnage. Long day produces more dry matter.
Soil Requirements:
Soil Type: Deep red soil, Black soil; well-drained loamy soils are ideal (ಆಳವಾದ ಕೆಂಪು ಮಣ್ಣು, ಕಪ್ಪು ಮಣ್ಣು).
Soil Ph: 6.0 to 7.5 (Optimum)
Drainage: Essential  -  water should not stagnate (ಅತ್ಯಗತ್ಯ  -  ನೀರು ನಿಲ್ಲಬಾರದು)
Notes: Saline and alkaline soils are not suitable.

CHUNK 18
CATEGORY: general
TOPIC: Planting Practices
SIZE: 1009
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Crop: Sugarcane (ಕಬ್ಬು / Saccharum officinarum L. Poaceae) | Topic: Planting Practices
Planting Methods:
Method: Furrow planting
Details: 15-20 cm deep furrows across slope at 90 cm spacing. Setts placed with buds facing sideward and covered with soil. In ridges/furrows, setts planted half way through ridge.

Method: Flat bed planting
Details: Shallow furrows 8-10 cm depth at 90 cm apart. 3 eye budded setts planted at 30 cm distance. Covered by running wooden plank and irrigated by flooding.

Method: Trench method
Details: Trenches 2-3 m long, 25-30 cm deep, 40 cm wide at 90 cm spacing. Suitable for coastal areas (prevents lodging) and stiff clays/sloppy lands.

Method: Pit method
Details: Square pits 40-60 cm dug at 90 cm apart. Trenches dug after every 3 rows to store water. Suitable for low rainfall areas. Two setts planted per pit (>5 cm depth).

Method: Paired row / Wide row
Details: 90 cm row spacing with 30 cm plant spacing. 120 cm gap can be used for mechanized farming or intercropping.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Crop: Sugarcane (ಕಬ್ಬು / Saccharum officinarum L. Poaceae) | Topic: Ratooning
Management Practices:
Trash Burning: Advised if plant crop is infested with scales/mealy bugs, termites, rodents, or excess moisture affecting sprouting.
Stubble Shaving: Cutting protruding stubbles to ground level with a spade to facilitate healthy underground bud sprouting and deep root system.
Off Barring: Breaking/cutting ridges on either side to loosen soil between rows, reducing compaction and aiding root development.
Gap Filling: Crucial if gaps >20%. Done using pre-germinated single eye bud setts or excess sprouts. Fill gaps with new seedlings.
Fertilizer Management: Entire P and 1/3 N&K applied soon after stubble shaving. 25% extra Nitrogen is recommended for ratoon crops.
Yield Notes: Ratoon yield is typically 15-20% lower than main crop. Recommended for maximum 2 cycles (ಎರಡನೇ ರಾಟೂನ್ ಗಿಂತ ಹೆಚ್ಚು ಬೇಡ).
